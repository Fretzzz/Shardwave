# Shardwave privacy policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Shardwave is a single-player puzzle game made by AT8 Studios. This policy
describes exactly what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores

Shardwave saves your game progress and preferences on your own device:

- best score for each mode, total runs, lines cleared and Prisms cleared;
- the date and score of your last Daily Shard, and your daily streak;
- whether sound and haptics are on.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not transmitted
anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in or profile.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
- No network access at all — the app does not request the `INTERNET`
  permission.
- No collection or sharing of personal data, location, contacts, files,
  purchase history or device identifiers.
- No in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Permissions

Shardwave requests one Android permission:

- `VIBRATE` — used for short haptic feedback when pieces lock and Prisms
  detonate. You can turn haptics off in the game menu.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds a feature that handles data (for example online
leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the
change will be described in the store listing notes.
